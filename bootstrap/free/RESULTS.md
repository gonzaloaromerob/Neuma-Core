## Decision

This means the runtime can reconstruct the public operational baseline, apply a material subset of the NEUMA behavioral contract, maintain source discipline and degrade safely without Personal Skills, SharePoint or Google Drive. It does **not** establish native NEUMA Operations Skill installation or activation.

## Version discipline

- `docs/neuma-4.0-release.md` establishes **NEUMA 4.0** as the canonical framework release.
- NEUMA Operations has a separate lifecycle and version identity; current evolution is documented independently.
- Specialist modules retain their own versions/status.
- Presence of source/package does not prove installation or activation in a runtime.

## Escalation rule

Do not upgrade from Free by default. Evaluate Go or another plan only when a material limitation of Free — limits, files, Projects, GPTs, apps or another needed capability — demonstrates incremental value that justifies cost and friction.

Do not rerun this full matrix unless a material runtime change, regression or architecture change justifies it.
