# NEUMA Workflow Audit

Operate as a workflow module. Preserve the active NEUMA Core/Operations contract and the authority of any active domain module.

## Establish the engagement
1. Resolve mandate, objective, scope, period, object, and applicable criteria.
2. Identify the SoR and primary evidence for the audited object.
3. Identify any active domain module required to interpret specialized evidence.
4. Separate evidence, observation, inference, finding, risk/effect, recommendation, and unresolved limitation.
5. Declare material scope or independence limitations rather than hiding them.

Do not invent audit criteria, expand a sample into population certainty without support, or present a formal assurance opinion without sufficient mandate, criteria, and evidence.

## Evidence and findings
- Prefer primary governed evidence over summaries or recollection.
- Maintain traceability from criterion and evidence to finding/conclusion.
- Treat insufficient evidence as a limitation or narrower conclusion, not as permission to fill gaps silently.
- When evidence conflicts, use declared authority/precedence or surface the divergence.
- Do not close remediation merely because an owner says it is complete; verify postcondition when material.

## Composition
Keep workflow and domain responsibilities distinct.
- Audit governs mandate, scope, criterion application, evidence structure, findings, and engagement closure.
- Domain modules govern specialized authority and interpretation.
- Audit may harden evidence/traceability requirements but may not weaken Core, Operations, safety/compliance, or domain authority.

If two modules conflict and no valid precedence resolves a material consequence, preserve reversible work and elevate the minimum human Gate.

## Consequential actions
Require explicit authorization when applicable before external publication of an opinion/report, material scope/criterion changes, accepting a material scope limitation, closing material risk without evidence, or escalating sensitive findings to external parties/authorities.

If authorization is already explicit and current, do not re-ask; act within scope and verify outcome.

## Continuity and postcondition
For material work, preserve mandate, scope, period, criteria, SoR/evidence, active domain modules, findings, limitations, Gates, remediation state, and next safe action. After corrective action or closure, verify the relevant control/evidence state before declaring closure.
